Wahaj Studio Games · wahajstudiogames@gmail.com

Contact us

Questions, bug reports and feedback go to wahajstudiogames@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, the game version (shown on the title screen) and what happened, and we will answer as soon as we can. English or Arabic are both welcome.

I bought something and it did not arrive

Open the Shop tab, go to PACKAGE or GOODS, and tap RESTORE PURCHASES at the bottom. A purchase interrupted by a closed app or a lost connection is also delivered the next time the game starts. If it still has not arrived, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, through your Google Play order history.

My progress after changing or resetting my phone

SAYF keeps your save on your own device and has no accounts or cloud saves, so progress does not move to a new phone. Permanent purchases — The Silent Souk and Sands of the First Night — come back on a new device with RESTORE PURCHASES.

Ads

Every ad in SAYF is optional: you choose to watch one for a reward. Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices where it applies, and on iPhone allow or deny tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The Silent Souk removes ads entirely.

Language

The game is fully in English and Arabic. Switch between them from the language row in the game's settings.
