Expenses – Privacy policy

Wahaj Studio · Effective 17 October 2026

The short version

Expenses does not collect, send or sell any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

  • The expenses, properties, leases, rent payments, investments and settings you enter are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no server. We cannot see your data.
  • Your iPhone may include the app's data in your own iCloud or computer backup, as it does for other apps. That backup belongs to you and is protected by Apple.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No connection to your bank or to Apple Pay. The optional Apple Pay import only reads a text file that a Shortcuts automation on your own iPhone writes, and only when you choose the file.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your iPhone for rent and payout dates. They are created on the device and are not sent through any server.

Files you share

Backups and spreadsheet exports are created only when you tap the button, and go only where you send them with the iPhone share sheet.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or delete the app. Both remove the data from your iPhone.

Children

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: wahajstudiogames@gmail.com.

سياسة الخصوصية (بالعربية)

لا يجمع تطبيق «المصاريف» أي بيانات عنك ولا يرسلها ولا يبيعها. كل ما تُدخله يبقى على جهاز iPhone الخاص بك.

  • تُحفظ المصاريف والعقارات وعقود الإيجار والدفعات والاستثمارات والإعدادات داخل التطبيق على جهازك فقط.

  • لا توجد تحليلات أو إعلانات أو تتبّع، ولا وصول إلى جهات الاتصال أو الموقع أو الصور أو الكاميرا أو الميكروفون.
  • استيراد Apple Pay اختياري، ويقرأ فقط ملفًا نصيًا يكتبه اختصار على جهازك، وعندما تختار الملف بنفسك.
  • التذكيرات إشعارات تُنشأ على جهازك فقط.
  • لحذف بياناتك: الإعدادات ← مسح كل البيانات، أو احذف التطبيق.
