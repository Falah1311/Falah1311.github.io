Expenses & more

Spending, rent and payouts · Wahaj Studio

Your spending, your rent cheques and your investment payouts in one simple, private iPhone app.

Search for “Expenses & more” on the App Store

Expenses

  • Add an expense in seconds: amount, category, date and how you paid.
  • See the month as a circle split by category, or as a calendar shaded by how much you spent each day.
  • Compare with last month at a glance.
  • Optional: record Apple Pay payments automatically with a Shortcuts automation, then approve them in the app.

Rent from your properties

  • Add buildings, villas, apartments, shops and offices, with as many units as you need.
  • Add a lease and the app builds its schedule of cheques or transfers.
  • See the next cheque due and what is overdue, and mark each payment claimed with one tap.

Investments

  • Track profit-share investments: the expected payout for each period, the withdrawal notice date and when your money comes back.
  • Record what actually arrived.

Reminders

A notification at 9:00 on each due date, and a few days before.

Private by design

  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
  • Back up to Files or iCloud Drive, and restore on a new phone.
  • Export to a spreadsheet (CSV).

In English and Arabic. Amounts in UAE dirhams (AED).
