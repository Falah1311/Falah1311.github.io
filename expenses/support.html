Back up and move your data

Your data lives only on your iPhone. In Settings, tap Back up now and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On a new iPhone, open Settings → Restore from a backup and pick that file.

Reminders

The app reminds you at 9:00 on each rent payment's due date and each expected profit payout, and a few days before (change this in Settings). If no reminders arrive, check that notifications are on for Expenses in the iPhone Settings app.

Apple Pay import

Optional. A Shortcuts automation writes each Apple Pay payment to a text file, and the app imports it for you to approve. The steps are in the app: Settings → Set up the Apple Pay shortcut.

Language

English or Arabic: Settings → Language.

Privacy

Nothing you enter leaves your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
