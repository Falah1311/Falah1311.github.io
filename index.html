Hisn Al-Sahra: Desert Fortress

Hold the last fortress of the sands — a pixel tower-defense roguelite.

The dunes are endless, and so are the raiders. Man the walls, rain arrows on every foe, and rise stronger with every run. Quick runs, deep permanent progression, hand-crafted pixel art — and no forced ads, ever.

Get it on Google Play

What's inside

  • Ten desert lands, each with its own hazard — sandstorms, heat, rockfalls
  • The Majlis: a night-sky wheel of permanent upgrades across four constellations
  • The Caravan Road: a five-stop journey where your whole build travels with you
  • Relics, blessings, dark omens, a daily trial, and a bestiary of eleven foes
