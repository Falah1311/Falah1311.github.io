SAYF: Blade of the Sands — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Publisher: Wahaj Studio Games — wahajstudiogames@gmail.com

SAYF: Blade of the Sands is a single-player idle role-playing game for Android and iPhone. This policy explains what the game handles, what leaves your device, and what you can do about it.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. SAYF has no accounts, no login, no analytics and no servers of our own. Your progress — levels, stages, gold, jawhar, equipment, relics — is written to a save file on your own device and never sent anywhere. Deleting the game, or using RESET SAVE in the settings, erases it permanently. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

What third parties collect

Google AdMob serves the optional rewarded ads — the ones you choose to watch in exchange for jawhar, dinars or a doubled chest — on Android and on iPhone. To do that, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or on iPhone Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from your IP address, diagnostic data, and information about the ads you were shown and how you interacted with them. It is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Before the first ad request the game shows Google's consent message where the law requires one (the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), and on iPhone it asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier can be used. If you decline either, ads are still available, but without the identifier or the personalisation you declined. The consent choice can be changed at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Players who buy The Silent Souk see no ads at all.

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone. Payment details are handled entirely by Google or Apple and are never seen by us; the game receives only a confirmation that a purchase was made, which it uses to deliver the goods and to restore permanent purchases on a new device.

Notifications

If you turn on NIGHT REMINDERS in the settings and allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your own device — for example when the night's chest is full. They are set and delivered locally by your phone; nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Children

SAYF is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • On Android, reset the advertising identifier or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.
  • On iPhone, allow or deny tracking for SAYF at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change or withdraw ad consent in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices (shown where consent is required).
  • Remove ads entirely by purchasing The Silent Souk.
  • Turn reminders off with NIGHT REMINDERS, or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Erase everything the game stores by using RESET SAVE, or by uninstalling.
